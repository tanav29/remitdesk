## Evidence and calculation

Keep one private row per transfer. Record the integration ID, provider transfer ID, mainnet transaction signatures, settlement time in UTC, anonymized payer, invoice reference, invoiced amount, delivered amount, collected sponsor percentage-fee share in USD, fee source and refund status. Attach a redacted invoice and enough delivery evidence to substantiate the payment. Share identifying records privately with the review team. [Sponsor evidence FAQ](https://business.multihopper.com/build-and-route/)

1. Match the integration and provider IDs to MultiHopper's attribution report. Check the signature and delivered amount against mainnet, then check the invoice and settlement time against the campaign window.
2. Deduplicate on provider transfer ID. Remove test, free, failed, refunded, circular and artificial payments. Record exclusions so the reviewer can reproduce the total.
3. Sum the sponsor's collected percentage-fee share on eligible transfers, excluding network and per-hop costs, and divide by 0.0025. For example, $20,000 principal at the full 0.5% rate produces $100 in percentage fees. The sponsor's $50 share yields $20,000 qualifying volume. On the same principal, a discounted $25 sponsor share yields $10,000. Use actual collected fees in the entry. [Sponsor calculation](https://business.multihopper.com/build-and-route/)
4. Group completed payments by payer organization and week. Report weekly volume, paying organizations, invoices settled and organizations with a second distinct settled invoice. Include the raw weekly counts when the preceding week had zero customers.
