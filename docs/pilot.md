# RemitDesk pilot

Recruit a finance operator who already pays independent vendors in Solana USDC. Start with their next approved invoice. The pilot succeeds for the customer if the vendor receives the agreed amount and the operator can reconcile it to the bill without chasing a transaction signature.

## Where to find the first operators

- **Design and development shops.** Ask a studio founder for an introduction to the person paying independent designers or developers. Portal has publicly commissioned UI/UX work in USDC. Its historical listing provides a concrete example of the buying pattern. [Portal design engagement](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/uiux-design-3)
- **Event and community operators.** Ask a local chapter lead for an introduction to a team that pays a production crew, venue or creative vendor in USDC. Superteam India's October design program offers a relevant starting point for introductions. Treat every introduction as a prospect until the operator confirms a payable. [Superteam India design program](https://superteam.fun/earn/listing/redesign-your-favourite-app-on-solana/)

**Finance lead draft**

> Hi [name], [introducer] mentioned you handle vendor payments at [team]. When a contractor asks whether their USDC invoice has cleared, how do you match their bill to the wallet transfer? I'm building RemitDesk for that handoff. Would you walk me through a recent payment using a redacted invoice? If it fits, we could review your next scheduled bill together. You see the fees and your team controls signing.

**After a pilot payment**

> Did [invoice reference] arrive for the amount your vendor expected? Could you reconcile it using the receipt alone? I'd like to know what was missing. With your permission, may we privately show the reviewer a redacted invoice and confirmation of delivery? If you have another bill where this would help, when is it due?

## Weekly pipeline

For each consenting prospect, track payer alias, next due date, expected amount, fee acceptance, wallet compatibility, actual settlement and follow-up owner. Keep a separate total for scheduled invoices. As a planning example, five independent payers with two genuine $10,000 bills each would reach $100,000 at full fees if all ten settle within the window. No such payers or invoices have been established here.
